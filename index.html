Program Overview

Everything your team needs, nothing you don’t.

The Collective Coaching Internship Program connects health tech companies with clinicians eager to apply real-world expertise outside traditional clinical roles.

Affordable Talent

Vetted, motivated clinicians available for 3 or 6-month engagements at tiered rates matched to your company’s growth stage.

Customizable Projects

We work with you to define scope, required skills, and cultural fit — ensuring your intern contributes meaningfully from day one.

Leadership Check-ins

Monthly tripartite meetings between your team, the intern, and a Collective Coaching mentor keep everyone aligned and progressing.

Collective Coaching interns are clinicians (OTs, PTs, SLPs, PAs, and more) pivoting into health tech — they bring hands-on patient care experience to projects across product, research, operations, and growth. Below are examples of real project types our interns have tackled.

Product Research & Testing

Clinicians evaluate product features, surfaces usability gaps, and validate workflows against real clinical scenarios — giving your team authentic end-user perspective before launch.

Market Validation

Interns conduct landscape analysis, competitive research, and stakeholder interviews — surfacing clinical insights that shape go-to-market strategy and positioning for health tech products.

Customer Success Workflows

Interns build onboarding resources, support documentation, and clinical training materials — helping health tech companies improve adoption and reduce churn with clinician-informed content.

Clinical Content & User Engagement

From evidence-based blog content to patient-facing education and in-app copy, interns create materials that resonate with clinical audiences and meet regulatory and accuracy standards.

GTM & Implementation Support

Interns assist with sales enablement, clinical objection handling, pilot program support, and implementation workflows — bridging the gap between your product and your clinical buyers.

Clinical Operations

Interns support virtual care workflows, clinical program coordination, and operational processes — helping companies scale clinical services without the overhead of a full-time hire.

Dedicated Collective Coaching Mentor

Every intern is paired with an experienced health tech clinician who provides biweekly professional coaching throughout the engagement.

Rigorous Vetting

Every Collective Coaching intern is screened for clinical credentials, communication skills, and professional readiness before placement.

Progress Tracking

Structured check-ins, defined milestones, and a formal close-out session ensure your project goals are met and outcomes are documented.
